Designed for Central Park, this pop-up kiosk for Pecann crafts an inviting, artisanal bakery aesthetic around an existing structural pillar. The design wraps the central column in warm timber trim, off-white panels, and light sage green tiling, transforming a structural constraint into a prominent vertical branding feature.   

The operational floor plan utilizes smooth, rounded wooden counters with frosted glass panels and built-in under-lighting. The front houses a low-profile glass display case for fresh baked goods, while the adjacent counter integrates a POS ordering terminal and vertical digital screen for dynamic menu presentation. delivering a compact, highly functional retail layout within the open mall corridor.
