Designed for the gourmet hall floor at EmQuartier, this kiosk for Adam n Eve translates a playful, health-conscious dessert concept into an open, high-visibility retail structure. The layout utilizes a bold chartreuse overhead frame supported by slim vertical posts, incorporating bright pixelated LED typography and overhead spotlights.   

The lower base combines textured stucco-style white panels with custom stamp graphics and warm under-lighting. Functionally divided into distinct "Order Here" and "Pick Your Toppings" service zones, the counter houses an integrated soft-serve machine, glass-shielded topping displays, and an eye-level vertical digital menu board to streamline customer flow.
