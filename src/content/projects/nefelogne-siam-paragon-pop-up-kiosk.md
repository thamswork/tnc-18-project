Designed for an open-plan retail zone at Siam Paragon, this island kiosk balances modern industrial finishes with refined organic details. The main service counter is defined by vertical metallic slats and a warm, glowing backlit brand sign, creating an immediate visual anchor for shoppers navigating the floor.

The layout incorporates a fluid, low-profile display surface that keeps products easily accessible while maintaining clean sightlines across the corridor. To the side, a soft, fabric-clad lantern structure introduces a warm, sculptural element that complements the metallic framing and completes the intimate, high-end retail atmosphere.
