Designed for a major fragrance launch at One Bangkok, this 160 sqm event setup transforms the open hall into a bold, pop-art experience. The space centers on an angled runway with graphic yellow and blue floor lining that leads directly to a main stage backdrop framed by giant Moschino bear perfume bottles.

Rows of low-profile, black modular ottomans line the catwalk, providing structured seating while preserving open sightlines. The surrounding floor plan incorporates colorful cylindrical display pedestals, interactive testing counters, and dedicated photography setups in bright pink, cyan, and yellow tones that align with the brand’s playful identity.
