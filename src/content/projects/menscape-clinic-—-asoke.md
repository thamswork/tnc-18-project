Spanning 455 square meters in the heart of Asoke, Bangkok, this clinic construction and interior fit-out for Menscape Clinic seamlessly combines clinical-grade medical standards with high-end hospitality design. The expansive space is engineered to deliver discreet, full-spectrum patient care while offering a sleek, reassuring aesthetic tailored for men’s health, wellness, and aesthetic treatments.

The main reception area centers around a customized modern counter with integrated blue brand typography, surrounded by durable matte wall paneling and indirect cove lighting. Across the 455 sq.m. floor plan, the layout integrates private consultation offices, advanced therapy rooms, and high-spec surgical procedure zones. Clean architectural lines, flush storage cabinetry, and seamless hygienic flooring ensure strict compliance with healthcare safety standards without compromising on patient comfort and privacy.
