Designed for the brand's milestone location at Central Rama 3, this dessert shop interior balances a warm, sweet atmosphere with practical retail functionality. The layout features custom display cabinetry designed to showcase signature cakes, pastries, and exclusive treats like Carabite items at eye level.



The spatial design utilizes soft, welcoming tones and clean illumination to create an eye-catching storefront along the mall corridor. Every square meter is planned for efficient customer queuing and smooth staff operations, resulting in a cohesive, brand-aligned retail experience that welcomes shoppers in for specialty desserts.
