A playful pop-up store for fashion brand Stylist at Central Westgate. Two oversized pastel-pink doors frame the booth, opening onto a soft pink polka-dot backdrop with the illuminated Stylist logo above. A walnut-finish display counter on cylindrical legs anchors the space, with white tiles and pink accents for showcasing shoes, bags, caps and accessories, all set on a rounded pink floor platform.
